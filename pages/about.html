Who I Am

Building AI Systems That Work Beyond the Proof-of-Concept

I'm Geeta Kakrani — an Enterprise AI Consultant, Google Developer Expert in AI/ML, and international speaker who has helped organizations ranging from venture-backed startups to large enterprises design, build, and ship production-grade AI systems.

My work sits at the intersection of deep technical architecture and strategic business thinking. I don't just advise on AI — I design the systems, write the architecture docs, and often build the proof-of-concept that gets teams unstuck and moving forward.

With hands-on expertise across Generative AI, LLMs, Vertex AI, Agentic AI, RAG systems, LangChain, and LangGraph — and a track record of 30+ conference talks across 8 countries — I bring both the technical depth and communication clarity that complex AI initiatives require.
