Who I Am

Teaching AI Systems Beyond the Proof-of-Concept

I'm Geeta Kakrani — a Google Developer Expert in AI/ML, blogger, startup advisor, and creator of developer resources. As the founder of Kanishka IT, I lead developer bootcamps, advise early-stage founders on AI roadmap execution, and help engineering teams design production-ready, scalable AI architectures.

My core mission is to make complex intelligence architectures accessible. I break down advanced AI research into working code, guides, and codelabs that developer communities can run locally and scale to production.

With expertise in Generative AI, Vertex AI, Agentic workflows, and semantic search systems, I have delivered over 30 conference talks across 8 countries, sharing practical engineering knowledge.
