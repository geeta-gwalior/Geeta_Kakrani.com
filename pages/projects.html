Legal AI · AI-TPU Specialization

LexiMini — AI Legal Assistant for India, Built on Google TPU

The problem: Indian legal professionals had no AI tool trained on Indian law — generic LLMs hallucinate extensively on IPC, CrPC, and case law. The challenge was domain adaptation with limited compute budget.

Approach

Fine-tuned a Gemma base model on a curated Indian legal corpus using Google Cloud TPU v4 pods — dramatically reducing fine-tuning cost vs GPU. Built a retrieval layer over Indian Kanoon case law with PGVector for grounded responses. Deployed on Vertex AI with custom evaluation pipeline to track hallucination rate.

Key Outcome

✅ TPU fine-tuning cost 60–70% less than equivalent GPU run. Domain-specific accuracy significantly outperformed base Gemini on Indian legal queries. Demonstrated viability of specialized legal AI for Indian market at accessible cost.

AI Cost Optimization

Hybrid Multi-Model Infrastructure to Cut AI Costs by 60%

The problem: Engineering teams routing all queries to Gemini 2.5 Pro — overkill for 70% of tasks, driving up costs with no accuracy benefit.

Approach

Designed a semantic complexity router that classifies incoming queries and dispatches: low-complexity tasks to Gemma 3 on Vertex AI (2B model), high-reasoning tasks to Gemini 2.5 Flash. Routing logic built with LangGraph, latency monitored via custom Cloud Logging dashboard.

Outcome

✅ ~60% reduction in API costs. Response quality maintained or improved for complex tasks. Architecture now used as reference implementation for cost-efficient LLM routing.

On-Device AI · Privacy

Fully Offline AI Chatbot on Android — No Server, No Cloud

The problem: Healthcare and legal apps need AI inference without sending sensitive user data to cloud APIs. On-device AI was the only viable path.

Approach

Deployed Gemma 2B on Android using Google MediaPipe LLM Inference API, integrated into a Flutter app with a native chat UI. Quantized model to INT4 for device memory constraints. No network calls during inference — full data sovereignty.

Outcome

✅ Sub-2s first token on mid-range Android. Complete user privacy — zero data leaves the device. Accompanied by public codelab used in GDE workshops.

Agentic AI · Research Automation

AI Research Workflow Automation with On-Premise Models

The problem: Research teams processing sensitive internal documents couldn't use cloud APIs. Needed fully local AI research automation.

Approach

Configured OpenClaw with Docker, connected to a local Ollama runtime running Gemma 3. Built document ingestion pipeline, semantic search over internal corpus, and automated report synthesis — entirely air-gapped. No data leaves the company network.

Outcome

✅ Full on-premise AI research pipeline. Sensitive data never leaves internal network. Cuts research synthesis time from hours to minutes.

Agentic AI · Multi-Agent

Production Multi-Agent System with Specialized AI Roles

The problem: Single LLM calls fail for complex multi-step tasks that need planning, research, writing, and review — each requiring different reasoning modes.

Approach

Built a CrewAI + Gemini architecture with four specialized agents: Planner (task decomposition), Researcher (web + corpus retrieval), Writer (structured output), Reviewer (quality gate). Each agent has distinct tools, memory, and termination criteria.

Outcome

✅ Measurably higher output quality vs single-agent on complex tasks. Reusable framework now used as template for 3 corporate AI projects.
