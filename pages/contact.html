FAQ

Common Questions

What size companies do you work with?

I work with early-stage startups (10+ people) through large enterprises (10,000+ employees). What matters more than size is that you have a real problem and genuine commitment to solving it with AI.

Do you work with international clients?

Yes — I work remotely with clients across India, Southeast Asia, the Middle East, Europe, and North America. For workshops and on-site consulting, travel can be arranged.

How long does a typical engagement take?

AI Readiness Assessments take 1–2 weeks. Strategy consulting is typically 2–4 weeks. Full system builds range from 6 weeks to 4 months depending on scope and complexity.

Do you offer ongoing retainer advisory?

Yes — monthly advisory retainers are available for companies that need ongoing AI guidance, architecture reviews, and technical decision support after an initial engagement.

What information helps for the discovery call?

Come with your business problem, current tech stack, team size, and what success looks like. You don't need to have a technical spec — we'll figure that out together.

Can you speak at our conference or event?

Absolutely. I'm available for keynotes, technical talks, panel discussions, and workshops. Use the form above and select "Conference Speaking" — include your event date and theme.
